# The 5 pillars of islam

The five pillars give worship a steady rhythm: the testimony of faith, the daily prayers, charity, fasting in Ramadan, and pilgrimage for those able to make it.

## More than a checklist

Each pillar trains a different part of the person. Together they join belief, time, wealth, restraint, community, and remembrance into a life directed toward Allah.
