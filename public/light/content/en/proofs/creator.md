# Existence of a creator; Allaah

Everything dependent points beyond itself. The universe changes, begins, follows intelligible order, and contains things that could have been otherwise. These are not the qualities of an ultimate explanation.

## From creation to the Creator

This page will build the case carefully: why a necessary, independent, knowing, and willing Creator best explains the existence of everything else—and why that Creator is Allaah.
