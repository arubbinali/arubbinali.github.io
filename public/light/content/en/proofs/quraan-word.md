# The Quraan is the word of Allaah

The Quraan presents itself not merely as inspired reflection, but as revelation from the Creator. That claim can be examined through its preservation, language, message, historical setting, internal coherence, and effect.

## A cumulative case

No single slogan will carry the argument. This page will gather the strands and ask which explanation makes the best sense of the text before us.
